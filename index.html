SUPPORT

Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?
Estimated delivery time is 7–21 days from purchase confirmation, depending on the store, the international carrier schedule, and customs processing. Full details are in our Shipping Policy.
Do I need an international credit card?
No. Try Shoppy buys the product on your behalf and you pay us in Egypt — Cash on Delivery, InstaPay, Visa, Mastercard, PayPal or bank transfer.
Does the price include shipping and customs?
Yes. The calculator combines the item price with the applicable shipping and customs into a single EGP price, shown to you before you confirm the order.
Where do I find my order number?
Every Try Shoppy order number looks like TRY12345678 — TRY followed by 8 digits. You receive it when your order is confirmed, and you can enter it on the Track Order page at any time.
Can I cancel an order?
Cancelling before we purchase the item is free of charge. After purchase, the terms in our Cancellation Policy apply.
Are any products restricted?
Yes — some categories cannot be shipped for compliance reasons. Please check the Restricted Items list before ordering.
